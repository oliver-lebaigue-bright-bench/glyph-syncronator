Under the hood

Audio in. Light out. Twenty milliseconds.

A deterministic pipeline — no randomness, no guessing. Every frame is pure math from the spectrum.

01
Capture
Media projection, mic, or Shizuku. Your audio stream, nothing else.
02
FFT
20ms windows. Spectrum split into frequency bins every frame.
03
Map
Each glyph zone listens to its own Hz range via zones.config.
04
Smooth
Downward-only decay. Snappy attack, clean fade. No jitter.
05
Drive
Glyph LEDs, haptics, flashlight — all at full hardware depth.
Capabilities

Built for people who notice the difference.

Per-zone frequency mapping

Bass hits the camera ring. Mids light the slash. Highs dance on the battery bar. Every segment has its own Hz range.

12-bit brightness

4,096 discrete levels instead of three. Soft glows, hard kicks, and everything between — actually visible.

Haptics & flashlight

Any Android phone. Bass-driven continuous motor, beat-detection pulses, multi-intensity flashlight modes.

Custom presets

Edit zones.config. Share community presets. Bass-only, zebra, spectrum, vocal — or design your own.

Latency compensation

Bluetooth lag? Dial it in. Auto-measure with the mic wizard. Glyphs hit with the beat, not after it.

Nothing-native UI

Dot-matrix typography. OLED black. Material You and Nothing themes. Widgets, QS tiles, nav-bar overlay viz.

Compatibility

Full Glyph support across the lineup.
